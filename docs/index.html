Flashcard - Create. Learn. Pass.

A fast, clean, and 100% offline study app for Android. Create custom decks, organize with tags, and master your study material with instant search, batch editing, and smart progress tracking.

Coming soon on Google Play

Features

Full Content Management

Create, edit, and organize decks, flashcards, and custom tags through a clean, consistent bottom-sheet interface.

100% Offline-First

Study anywhere without an internet connection. All decks, cards, and learning statistics are stored reliably on your device.

Smart Study & Progress

Practice with interactive learning sessions and track completion percentages and remaining cards per deck at a glance.

Rich Media Cards

Attach images to flashcards for visual learning, powered by fast local image loading and caching.

Instant Search & Batch Edit

Find cards immediately with reactive search and sorting, or use multi-select mode to batch-edit large study decks seamlessly.

Modern Material 3 UI

Built natively with Jetpack Compose and MVI architecture, featuring smooth animations and intuitive swipe-to-action gestures.
